## Containment and receipts

The outer launch-driver owner alone creates the managed POSIX group or Windows Job Object before releasing its bridge. Inner command bridges inherit that boundary and record direct exit/reaping/EOF; they do not claim independent whole-tree quiescence. On Linux their observed process-group IDs must equal the outer group. Native Windows uses no breakaway flags and final outer accounting must reach active=0 with normal exit and both pipe EOF/readers joined.
